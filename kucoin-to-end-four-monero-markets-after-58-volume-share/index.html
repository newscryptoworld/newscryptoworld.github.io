KuCoin to End Four Monero Markets After 58% Volume Share

KuCoin will close four Monero spot pairs on Nov. 30 after they handled about 58% of tracked XMR volume, while deposits remain shut and withdrawals stay open.

Crypto Record EditorialNov 27, 2026 · 15:30 UTC3 min read

KuCoin will stop spot trading in Monero (XMR) on Nov. 30, closing four markets that handled about 58% of XMR volume tracked across venues in a recent 24-hour period. An exchange pairs buyers and sellers in an order book for each market; when a pair closes, traders can no longer place or match orders there. KuCoin says it will also remove open orders after trading ends.

The exchange’s Nov. 26 delisting notice names XMR/USDT, XMR/USDC, XMR/ETH and XMR/BTC. Each pair lets users trade XMR against a different asset: two stablecoins, ether or bitcoin. All four are scheduled to close at 08:00 UTC on Nov. 30. KuCoin cited its platform policies but gave no further reason in the notice.

The volume share comes from Unchained’s report and calculation. In the 24 hours to 07:30 UTC on Nov. 27, the four KuCoin markets recorded about $105 million in volume. CoinGecko recorded $182.5 million across all venues in the same period, putting KuCoin’s share at roughly 58%, according to the report. About 22 hours of that measurement window fell after KuCoin’s notice was published. The figure describes that period’s recorded trading; it does not establish how volume will shift after the pairs close.

The notice sets different deadlines for other XMR services. KuCoin says deposits are already closed and will not resume. Its XMR Flexible Savings product is scheduled to end at 12:00 UTC on Nov. 27, with users’ principal and accrued earnings transferred to their Funding Accounts. The exchange’s Convert feature will stop accepting XMR at 03:00 UTC on Nov. 30, and its XMR trading bots will be delisted at 06:00 UTC that day.

After spot trading ends, KuCoin says XMR withdrawals are scheduled to remain available until 08:00 UTC on Dec. 31. It reserves the right to close withdrawals sooner if project-related problems arise. XMR still in accounts after withdrawals close may be converted into stablecoins, but KuCoin says that conversion is not guaranteed. From Nov. 30, the exchange will also stop accepting XMR as collateral for VIP Loans.

The immediate change is to where KuCoin customers can trade XMR: after the four order books close, they will no longer be able to buy or sell the coin on those markets. The reported volume share shows those pairs accounted for a substantial portion of tracked XMR trading during the measured day. It does not, by itself, show whether other venues can absorb that activity.
