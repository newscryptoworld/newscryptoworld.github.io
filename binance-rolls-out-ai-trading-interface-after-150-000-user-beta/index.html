Binance rolls out AI trading interface after 150,000-user beta

Binance has opened its free AI interface to English-language users after a beta of about 150,000, adding personalized market context and portfolio tools.

Crypto Record EditorialOct 13, 2026 · 10:53 UTC2 min read

Binance has rolled out its free Binance AI interface to global English-language users after a beta with about 150,000 testers, giving traders personalized market context and portfolio features inside the exchange. The interface changes what information users see based on their interests and experience, according to Binance’s Oct. 12 announcement.

How does Binance AI change the trading screen?

Binance AI uses generated layouts and widgets to tailor the information on screen. Users can choose which widgets appear, while the interface can adapt its modules to the assets they explore. Binance says newer users may see simpler explanations and prompts; active traders can access deeper market context and data views.

Think of it as a customizable dashboard that rearranges its panels around what a user follows. The named features include a recap of the previous day’s performance, analysis of 24-hour moves in a user’s holdings, voice alerts for tracked assets, and contextual tips on product pages. A market brief offers on-demand updates in text or audio and refreshes every four hours, Binance says.

What did the beta establish, and who can use it?

The beta began on Sept. 7, and about 150,000 users tested the product, a Binance spokesperson told The Block. Binance’s beta announcement invited verified users to register, with places granted on a rolling basis. The Oct. 12 rollout opens the interface to global English-language users; additional language support is still rolling out, according to The Block.

Binance says the app’s AI features begin adapting to a user’s activity after updating to the latest version. The company also says the experience is rolling out progressively to eligible users on its app and website, so availability may depend on eligibility and region.

Does the interface make trades for its users?

Binance’s announcement describes market information, portfolio summaries, alerts, educational prompts and customizable widgets. It does not describe Binance AI as an autonomous trading agent or say that it places orders for users. The product’s stated function is to organize and explain information within Binance’s trading experience.

That distinction matters because a market summary or alert can shape a user’s next decision without making it for them. Binance says its AI outputs may contain errors or outdated information and should not be relied on alone for decisions. Users remain responsible for their investment choices, the company says.
