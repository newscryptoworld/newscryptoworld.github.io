About Chain News

Chain News is an independent analysis desk covering the crypto industry as a whole: protocol upgrades, exchanges and infrastructure, stablecoins, market structure and regulation. Each story sets the news against what came before and against the alternatives, then names what to watch next.

We work from primary sources such as filings, code repositories, official statements and on-chain records, and we separate what is confirmed from what is reported. Errors are corrected in the article with a dated note. We are independent of every project we cover, and nothing here is financial advice.
