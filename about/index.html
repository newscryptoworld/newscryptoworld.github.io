About Crypto Record

Crypto Record reports news from across the crypto market: Bitcoin and Ethereum, newer networks such as Solana, DeFi protocols, exchanges, stablecoins, security incidents and the regulation that shapes them. Each story sets out how the thing in the news works: the parts involved, the steps, and what changed.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, token, exchange or fund we cover. Stories are built from primary sources such as project documentation, code repositories, on-chain records, filings and official statements. When we get something wrong, we correct it openly by updating the article and noting the change. Crypto Record is an independent publication, and nothing published here is financial advice.
