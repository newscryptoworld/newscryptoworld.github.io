About Crypto Daybook

Crypto Daybook reports news from across crypto: Bitcoin and Ethereum, other networks and their upgrades, exchanges and stablecoins, DeFi protocols, regulation and enforcement, and security incidents. Each story sets out how the thing in question works and what changed.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, token, exchange or company we cover. We work from primary sources such as filings, on-chain records, code repositories and official statements, and we correct errors openly by updating the article and noting the change. Crypto Daybook is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
