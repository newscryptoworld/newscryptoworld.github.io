What happens to my deposit if a vault is paused? How to exit

What happens to my deposit if a vault is paused? New deposits and compounding stop, but mooTokens remain redeemable for your share through withdrawal.

Crypto Record EditorialNov 28, 2026 · 04:05 UTC3 min read

A paused Beefy Vault stops accepting deposits and compounding, but you can still redeem your mooTokens through withdrawal.

Say the farm behind your vault has a problem and the strategy is paused. Your deposit does not disappear: the mooTokens in your wallet still represent your share, and the vault’s withdraw function remains available. You can use it to redeem that share for the underlying vault asset.

A pause leaves your share redeemable

When you deposit into a Beefy Vault, the vault issues mooTokens that record your proportional share. To withdraw, you return mooTokens to the vault. It burns them and transfers your share of the underlying asset back to you.

During a strategy pause, the deposit and harvest paths are stopped, while withdrawal remains callable. That distinction matters: you cannot add funds or rely on the strategy to keep collecting and compounding rewards, but you can still exit by redeeming your mooTokens.

A pause can follow the end of a farm, a problem in the underlying protocol, or an issue with the strategy. In an emergency, the strategy’s panic() function withdraws funds from the farm and brings them back to the strategy contract. The pause helps prevent further strategy activity while the issue is addressed.

Think of it as moving your share out of a working machine and into a safe waiting area. The farm is no longer generating rewards for the strategy, but the assets backing your share can still be redeemed through the vault.

That is the mechanism behind a paused vault, and it can help when weighing how to reduce Beefy app risks before depositing. Understanding those risks helps you decide which vault and underlying protocol fit your situation; it does not change how withdrawal works once you hold mooTokens.

Beefy’s documentation describes panic as a way to bring staked funds back to the strategy so users can withdraw. Its contract repository also includes a test that checks a user can withdraw after panic while a new deposit reverts. Those details support the practical takeaway: a pause stops the strategy’s normal work, not your ability to redeem your share.

A retired vault still has a withdrawal route

When a farm ends, Beefy may mark its vault as retired, or EOL. The deposit button disappears in the app, while the withdraw option remains. Your mooTokens still represent a claim on the vault’s underlying asset, so you can redeem them through withdrawal.

Retirement and a strategy pause are related but distinct. A pause changes what the strategy can do while the vault is paused. EOL status marks a vault as retired, so the app no longer offers new deposits. In either case, the stated withdrawal route remains available.

Once a strategy has panicked or stopped harvesting, the assets held for it sit idle rather than continuing through the farm’s reward cycle. Your share may remain redeemable, but it earns nothing from that strategy while it is paused. Waiting for the vault to resume means waiting without compounding during the pause.

For a depositor whose vault has paused, the practical move is to withdraw and, if appropriate, choose another vault. The Beefy app’s withdrawal flow exchanges your mooTokens for your share of the underlying vault asset. Check that you have the mooTokens in your wallet, select withdraw, and review the transaction before signing. You do not need to wait for the deposit button to return in order to use the available withdrawal route.
