Editorial policy

What we cover

Our beat is Crypto — exchanges, blockchains and protocols, policy and regulation, security incidents and the companies building in the space. We cover developments; we do not offer trading calls or price predictions.

Sourcing

Stories are built on primary and reputable sources: official announcements, filings, court records, on-chain data and established outlets. Where a story relies on a source, it links to it, and it separates what is confirmed from what is still unclear.

Independence

Our stories are written for readers, not for the projects they describe. A reference to any token, platform or service is information, not a recommendation.

Accuracy and corrections

We aim to get every detail right. If a factual error is found, we fix the story.

Information only

Our content is informational. It does not take anyone's personal circumstances into account and is not investment advice. Digital assets carry significant risk.
