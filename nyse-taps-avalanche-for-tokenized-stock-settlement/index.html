NYSE taps Avalanche for tokenized stock settlement

NYSE plans a 24/7 tokenized-securities venue using Avalanche for settlement while its existing system matches trades; AVAX rose as much as 10% on Nov. 9.

Crypto Record EditorialNov 23, 2026 · 15:45 UTC2 min read

The New York Stock Exchange plans to use Avalanche technology to settle trades on a new tokenized-securities platform, while NYSE’s existing system will match buyers and sellers. The platform is targeting a fourth-quarter launch, subject to regulatory approval, according to Avalanche’s announcement.

The process has two main steps. First, the NYSE’s trading technology matches orders. Then a blockchain ledger records and settles the trade. Think of it like a market’s order desk paired with a separate record book: one brings the two sides together, and the other logs that the transaction is complete.

A custom Avalanche-based ledger would handle settlement

Ava Labs chief business officer John Nahas told CoinDesk that the NYSE plans to run its own custom blockchain built with Avalanche technology. That means the settlement system would not use Avalanche’s public blockchain directly.

The planned venue is an alternative trading system, or ATS, affiliated with the NYSE. It is designed for around-the-clock trading and settlement. The platform may support tokenized stocks issued on different blockchains; the Avalanche-based ledger would connect to those networks to record and settle trades. The announcement describes settlement as instant, but the launch still depends on regulatory approval.

For readers, the distinction matters: Avalanche technology is slated for the back-end record of completed trades, while the platform’s trading system finds matches between orders. The reported selection does not mean every tokenized stock would be issued on Avalanche.

AVAX rose as much as 10% after the news

Avalanche’s native token, AVAX, gained as the announcement circulated. CoinDesk reported it was up 8% over the prior 24 hours. Cointelegraph reported that it rose as much as 10% on Nov. 9, then eased to $8.86, still more than 8% higher for the day as of 3:22 p.m. UTC.

That market move followed news of a planned use for Avalanche technology. It does not establish that the settlement platform will use AVAX as a transaction fee or that the project will create demand for the token. The announcement confirms a role for Avalanche’s ledger technology; it does not specify AVAX’s role in the platform.

The next concrete steps are regulatory approval and a launch. Until then, the system remains planned infrastructure: NYSE technology to match trades, a custom Avalanche-based ledger to settle them, and tokenized securities that may originate on multiple networks.
