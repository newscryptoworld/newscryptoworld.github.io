How to Check Whether a Mantle Bridge Transfer Arrived

A Mantle transfer is complete only when the destination chain records it; follow the bridge status and transaction hash to distinguish arrival from a pending relay.

Crypto Record EditorialSep 29, 20263 min read

To check whether a Mantle transfer arrived, find its transaction in the bridge history, then confirm the result on the destination chain. A transfer crosses two networks, so a successful transaction on the sending network is only the first step. The bridge must relay the message, and the receiving network must record the funds at the intended address.

Start with the wallet address that sent the transfer and the transaction hash shown in your wallet or bridge history. The hash identifies the on-chain transaction; the bridge history connects it to the cross-chain message. For a fuller walkthrough of the process, see Mantle Bridge. Keep the direction in mind: a deposit goes from Ethereum to Mantle, while a withdrawal goes from Mantle to Ethereum.

How do I check an Ethereum-to-Mantle deposit?

For a deposit, first check that the Ethereum transaction succeeded, then look for the relayed deposit and balance on Mantle. Open the transaction hash in an Ethereum explorer. A successful status means Ethereum accepted the deposit transaction; it does not by itself prove the tokens have arrived on Mantle. The bridge’s history can show whether the cross-chain message is still processing or has completed.

Next, switch your wallet to Mantle and check the receiving address in a Mantle explorer or the wallet’s token balance. Confirm the address and token match the transfer. If the bridge reports completion but the wallet shows no balance, check that the wallet is displaying Mantle and that the token is visible there. A token balance recorded at your address is stronger evidence of arrival than a wallet notification alone.

How do I check a Mantle-to-Ethereum withdrawal?

For a withdrawal, check the Mantle transaction first, then follow the bridge status until the Ethereum-side transfer is complete. The Mantle transaction records that you initiated the withdrawal. The bridge must then process the cross-chain message, and some withdrawals require a final claim on Ethereum. A successful Mantle transaction can therefore coexist with funds that have not yet appeared on Ethereum.

Use the bridge history to see whether the withdrawal is processing, ready for a claim, or complete. If a claim is required, the status should indicate when it can be submitted; after claiming, check the resulting transaction on Ethereum and confirm the balance at the destination address. The steps differ by direction because deposits are relayed onto Mantle, while withdrawals must be settled on Ethereum.

What should I check if the transfer is still pending?

Trace the transfer one stage at a time. A blockchain explorer shows whether a transaction was included and succeeded on that chain; bridge history shows the message’s progress between chains. Like a parcel with a dispatch scan and a delivery scan, a sending-chain confirmation proves departure, not arrival.

  • Verify the source and destination networks and the receiving address.
  • Check the transaction hash on the sending chain for success or failure.
  • Check bridge history for a pending relay or a withdrawal claim step.
  • Look up the receiving address and token on the destination chain.

If the source transaction failed, the transfer did not begin successfully. If it succeeded but the destination has no matching record, use the bridge’s status and the hash to identify the unfinished stage before retrying. Do not submit a second transfer just because the wallet balance has not refreshed. For any support request, share the transaction hash and public wallet address; never share a seed phrase or private key.
