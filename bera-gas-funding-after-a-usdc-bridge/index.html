BERA Gas Funding After a USDC Bridge

How to get BERA for gas after bridging USDC: send a small amount to your Berachain wallet, then use it for approvals and swaps while keeping a reserve.

Crypto Record EditorialNov 25, 2026 · 14:42 UTC4 min read

If USDC is visible in your Berachain wallet but you have no BERA, get a small amount of BERA before trying a swap or transfer. Berachain uses BERA to pay transaction gas, so USDC cannot cover the fee for its own transaction.

First, make sure MetaMask is connected to Berachain and that the USDC you bridged appears there. Confirm the token entry is the asset you intended to bridge. The USDC is what you can swap or use; BERA is what lets your wallet submit the transaction that moves it.

The Berachain bridge matters when choosing which asset to send across chains. If you still hold funds on the source chain and are weighing the two assets, read whether to bridge ETH or USDC to Berachain; if USDC has already arrived, the immediate task is getting BERA onto Berachain.

How can you get BERA into your Berachain wallet?

Choose a route that explicitly supports Berachain and delivers BERA or destination gas. The right option depends on whether you can access another wallet, use an exchange withdrawal, or select a bridge route with gas delivery.

  • Wallet transfer: Ask someone you trust, or use another wallet you control, to send a small amount of BERA to your Berachain address. Check the destination address and network before the sender confirms.
  • Exchange withdrawal: Use this route only if the exchange supports withdrawals on the Berachain network and the withdrawal asset is BERA. Select the network carefully; a token with the same ticker on a different chain will not pay Berachain gas.
  • Bridge route with gas delivery: Some routes may offer BERA or destination gas as part of the transfer. Read the route details and confirm what arrives on Berachain. A route that only delivers USDC will leave the gas problem unsolved.

Wait for the transfer to arrive, then check MetaMask is still set to Berachain and that the BERA balance is visible. This is like putting coins in a meter before using a service: the USDC is the value you want to move, while BERA pays the network to process the instruction.

Now try the intended action. A USDC swap may require an approval transaction first, which gives the chosen application permission to use the specified USDC amount. That approval is a transaction and uses BERA. After it confirms, submit the swap; that also uses BERA. A direct USDC transfer likewise needs BERA for its transaction fee.

You can swap only part of your USDC for BERA if a suitable route is available, but you need some BERA already to submit that swap. Keep enough BERA aside for later approvals, transfers, or swaps. The amount each action needs can vary with the transaction and network conditions, so check the wallet’s gas estimate and leave a reserve rather than spending the entire balance.

Change one detail: suppose the bridged asset is USDC, but it has not appeared in MetaMask yet. The next step is to confirm the bridge transfer and the destination network, then identify the correct token in the wallet. The gas rule stays the same: once you want to transact on Berachain, you need BERA, whether the asset arrived moments ago or earlier.

Related

Can I use USDC to pay gas on Berachain?

No. Berachain transactions that use USDC still require gas paid in BERA, including approvals and swaps.

How much BERA should I keep for gas?

There is no single amount that fits every transaction. Check the wallet’s estimate for the action you plan to take, then keep a BERA reserve for follow-up transactions. Send a small starter amount, complete the first approval or swap, and leave enough BERA to use the USDC again.
