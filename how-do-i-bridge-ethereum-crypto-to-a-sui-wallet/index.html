How do I bridge Ethereum crypto to a Sui wallet?

To bridge to Sui, create a Sui wallet, connect it and your Ethereum wallet to Suibridge, deposit a supported token, then wait for the matching token to appear.

Crypto Record EditorialNov 21, 2026 · 02:29 UTC4 min read

Install a Sui wallet first, because you need a destination address before you can send crypto from Ethereum. To bridge from Ethereum to Sui, connect that wallet and an Ethereum wallet to Suibridge, deposit a supported token, and wait for the matching token to be minted on Sui; Suibridge locks the deposit on Ethereum and mints an equal amount on Sui.

What do I need before bridging from Ethereum to Sui?

You need a Sui wallet, a supported token in an Ethereum wallet on Ethereum mainnet, and ETH in that Ethereum wallet to pay gas. Gas is the network fee for processing a transaction on Ethereum. Suibridge supports ETH, WBTC, USDT and LBTC from Ethereum mainnet. A closer look at where WBTC goes on Suibridge can help if that is the token you plan to send; Suibridge uses the same lock-and-mint process for supported assets.

Install the Sui wallet and follow its setup steps. Back up the recovery phrase somewhere private and secure: it gives access to the wallet. Then copy the wallet’s Sui address. This is where the minted token will arrive, so check that you have copied the address from the Sui wallet you intend to use.

In your Ethereum wallet, switch to Ethereum mainnet and make sure you hold both the token you want to bridge and enough ETH for the transaction fee. The amount of ETH needed depends on the gas cost when the transaction is processed. Keep some ETH aside for gas rather than assuming the token deposit will cover it.

How do I bridge crypto from Ethereum to Sui?

Use these steps to move the asset from your Ethereum wallet into the Sui wallet.

  1. Set up the Sui wallet. Create it, back up its recovery phrase, and copy its address. The wallet is ready to receive the bridged token.
  2. Connect both wallets to Suibridge. Connect the Ethereum wallet holding your asset and the Sui wallet you just created. Confirm that the displayed Sui address matches the one you copied. Both wallets are now selected for the transfer.
  3. Select a supported token and amount. Choose ETH, WBTC, USDT or LBTC from Ethereum mainnet, then enter how much to send. Review the destination and amount before continuing; the bridge will mint the matching token on Sui.
  4. Sign the required Ethereum transactions. For an ERC-20 token, such as WBTC or USDT, sign the token approval so Suibridge can handle the amount you selected. Then sign the deposit transaction. ETH does not require an ERC-20 approval. Your Ethereum wallet will show the transaction for confirmation.
  5. Wait for the mint on Sui. The bridge locks the deposited asset on Ethereum, then mints an equal amount on Sui. Wait for the process to finish and check the Sui wallet. The task is complete when the token balance appears there.

A small test transfer can confirm that the route reaches your Sui wallet, but it does not make the Ethereum deposit fee proportionally cheaper. Ethereum gas for the deposit does not scale with the amount sent: a small test costs the full fixed gas for that transaction, rather than a fraction of it. You pay that gas again for the later transfer. The trade-off is an extra fee in exchange for checking the route with a smaller amount; whether that check is worth the cost depends on your comfort with the wallet and destination details.

Do I need SUI tokens before bridging?

The listed bridge prerequisites are ETH for Ethereum gas, a supported Ethereum token, and a Sui wallet. SUI is not among those stated prerequisites for making the Ethereum deposit. The bridge sends the minted token to your Sui address; the information here does not establish what you may need for separate transactions on Sui afterward.

Can I bridge to Sui from an exchange instead?

This transfer starts with a supported token in an Ethereum wallet connected to Suibridge. If your crypto is still on an exchange, you need it in an Ethereum wallet on Ethereum mainnet before following these steps. Confirm the token and network when withdrawing, then use the wallet that holds the asset for the Ethereum connection.
