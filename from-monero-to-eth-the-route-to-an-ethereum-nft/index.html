From Monero to ETH: the route to an Ethereum NFT

Turn Monero proceeds into ETH by choosing a swap route, checking the receiving network and leaving enough ETH to pay the NFT purchase and its gas fee.

Crypto Record EditorialSep 29, 20263 min read

To buy an Ethereum NFT with Monero proceeds, convert XMR into ETH, send the ETH to a wallet on the NFT’s network, and use that wallet to pay. Monero and Ethereum run separate networks, so there is no shared balance to move between them. A conversion service or exchange has to receive the XMR and arrange for ETH to reach your wallet.

How does an XMR-to-ETH conversion work?

First, choose a route that accepts XMR and delivers ETH. A swap service quotes the amount and gives you a Monero address for the deposit; an exchange may instead credit your account after the XMR deposit clears, then let you trade XMR for ETH. In either case, the service is the link between the two networks. For a closer look at what to check before using one, see this guide to how an XMR bridge handles a conversion.

Next, send the quoted amount of XMR from your Monero wallet. The service detects the deposit, applies its exchange rate and fees, then sends ETH to the address you supplied or credits it to your exchange account. The quote can change or expire, and the amount delivered may be lower than the headline rate suggests once fees and any spread are included. Compare the final ETH amount you will receive, not just the displayed rate.

How do you get the ETH ready for an NFT purchase?

Withdraw or receive the ETH into a wallet you control, then check that it arrived on the same network the NFT listing uses. Wallet addresses can look alike across networks, but assets on one network do not automatically become assets on another. The marketplace’s listing and checkout screen should state the network and accepted payment method.

Leave some ETH unspent for gas. Gas pays for the network work needed to complete a transaction, such as transferring an NFT or approving a marketplace action. The amount varies with network activity and the transaction. A listing priced in ETH is not the total amount you need if the wallet balance covers only the listed price.

At checkout, connect the wallet, review the NFT and transaction details, and confirm the request in the wallet. The marketplace’s contract processes the purchase; the network records the resulting ownership change. Think of the conversion as exchanging cash at one counter before paying at another: each step has its own destination and fee.

Which route should you choose, and what should you check?

For many readers, an exchange with a visible XMR/ETH trade and a clear ETH withdrawal flow is easier to audit: the trade price, fees and withdrawal are shown as separate steps. A direct swap may require fewer actions, but its quote and delivery terms deserve the same scrutiny. Availability varies by service and location, so check that XMR deposits and ETH withdrawals are currently supported before sending funds.

  • Copy the receiving address from your own wallet, and verify it before submitting the conversion.
  • Confirm the ETH network matches the NFT marketplace’s network.
  • Check the net ETH after conversion and withdrawal fees, then reserve gas.
  • Send only after reviewing the deposit instructions and expected payout.

Monero transactions cannot be reversed once confirmed, and an incorrect destination or unsupported network can make funds difficult or impossible to recover. Start with the service’s exact instructions and keep the deposit, quote and withdrawal records. The practical sequence is simple: convert, verify the network, fund gas, then buy.
